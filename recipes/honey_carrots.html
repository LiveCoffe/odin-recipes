Hot Honey Glazed Carrots

Home

Description

Hot honey hot glazed carrots are simple to make. You only need 3 ingridients.

Ingridients

  • nonstick cooking spray
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3 tablespoons hot honey
  • pound baby carrots

Steps

  1. Preheat air fryer to 400 degrees F. Spray basket with nostick spray
  2. Combine butter and hot honey in a bowl; add carrots and toss to coat. Place carrots in the air fryer. Set bowl with remaining sause aside
  3. Toss carrots every 7 minutes, until carrots are roasted, about 15 to 22 minutes. Toss cooked carrots in remaining sause
