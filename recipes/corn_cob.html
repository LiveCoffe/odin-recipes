Corn on the Cob

Home

Description

Air fryed corn on the cob is quick and simple to make. You can add mayo, chili powder, lime juice, and cotija for added flavor.

Indegredients

  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 teaspoon crumbled cotija cheese
  • 1 teaspoons lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon chili powder
  • 2 ears corn, shucked and halved
  • 4 springs fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Preheat air fryer to 400 degrees
  2. Mix mayonnaise, cotija cheese, lime juice, and chili powder in a shallow dish.
  3. Roll each corn in mixutre until completely covered
  4. Place corn in air fryer. Cook until golden, crisp, and tender, about 8 minutes. Garnish with cilantro.
