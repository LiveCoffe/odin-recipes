Bagel Chicken Strips

Home

Description

Bagel chicken strips are easy to make. With only 3 simple ingredients, it only takes 5 minutes to prepare. An air-fryer is all you need and, it only takes 15 minutes to cook

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken tenders
  • 1 Tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/3 cup everything bagel seasoning

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients. Preheat air fryer to 400 degrees F
  2. Place tenders in a bowl. Add oil and toss to coat. Add everthing seasoning and toss again.
  3. Place tenders in the air fryer. Cook until no longer pink in center and juices run clear, about 12 minutes.
  4. Transfer to a serving plate
