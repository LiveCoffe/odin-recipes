Bagel Chicken Strips

Home

Description

Bagel chicken strips are easy to make. With only 3 simple ingredients, it only takes 5 minutes to prepare. An air-fryer is all you need and, it only takes 15 minutes to cook

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken tenders
  • 1 Tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/3 cup everything bagel seasoning
